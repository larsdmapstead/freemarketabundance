Privacy

Effective November 8, 2026. Lars Mapstead publishes freemarketabundance.com.

What we collect when you sign

Your email address, the name you enter, the time you signed, which form you used, the country your connection appears to come from, and your browser's user agent string. We use it to count signatures and to email you about this program.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not run ad trackers on this site.

Email

We write when there is something you can do about the program. You can ask to be removed at any time by writing to lars@mapstead.com, and we will delete your record.

Where it lives

Signatures are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Cloudflare keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.

Public numbers

We may publish the total number of signatures. We will never publish names or emails.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to lars@mapstead.com.
