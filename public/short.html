The two-minute version

Everything is about to get cheaper.

Your rent. Your power bill. Your doctor. Your kid’s school. AI and robots are about to make all of them cost a fraction of what they cost today.

You don’t need more money. You need the money you have to go further.

How

Labor and energy are the two biggest inputs in everything you buy. A $30,000 robot that works 100,000 hours is $5-an-hour labor. AI makes energy, medicine and learning cheap the same way. So every price falls. The check doesn’t get bigger. The world gets cheaper.

It has happened before. The tractor ended hunger in America. Reading a human genome cost $95 million in 2001 and about $525 in 2022. Nobody passed a law. Somebody built the next one, cheaper.

What you get

  • A home of your own, at a price you can pay. Built in months by robots, in a city that lets people build.

  • A doctor the same day, with the price on the door, and care that costs a fraction of today.

  • Power so cheap you stop checking the bill.

  • A class size of one for every kid, and friends for the rest of the day.

  • Work that is a choice, not a survival test. You keep what you earn.

  • A paycheck that goes further every year, because prices fall instead of rising.

Who pays? Nobody has to.

America already spends $5.3 trillion a year on health care, $2.5 trillion of it through government. Cut the cost of care by four fifths and insurance gets cheap enough for everyone to carry, the programs that exist today shrink as prices fall, and what government stops spending pays down the debt. Lower taxes, everyone covered, nobody taxed more. CMS, 2024

Why capitalism.

Every plan for abundance promises everyone lifted. Ours is the only one that gets there with nobody conscripted. Capitalism is free people building, trading and competing by choice, and it is the only system that has ever made things cheap. Free people produce more. That is the whole argument.
